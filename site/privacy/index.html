1. Who we are

Lumi is a spiritual consistency app: a short daily devotional, reading plans, challenges and prayer circles with friends.

The party responsible for your personal data (the controller) is [CONTROLLER NAME]. For any privacy matter, write to [CONTACT E-MAIL].

2. What data we process and why

  • Account. Your e-mail and the password you choose (the password is protected by the sign-in service; we never see it). If you sign in with YouVersion, we receive the e-mail of your YouVersion account. Used to create and access your account.
  • Profile. The name you choose, your age range, the reminder time, your time zone and your language. Used to personalize the app and the daily reminder.
  • Your activity. Devotionals started and finished, streak, XP, achievements, Lumi items, reading plans and challenges (including the ones you create).
  • Your reflections. The text you write at the end of the devotional is private: only you can see it. You can delete each reflection in your history. It is not part of the metrics and is not sent to artificial intelligence.
  • Circles. The circle name, your name as other members see it, the prayer requests you share and the "prayed" and "cheered" records. This is visible only to circle members. If someone reports content, we keep the report (who, what and why) for moderation.
  • Challenge invites. People doing the same challenge with you see your name and the challenge progress (a count only), nothing else.
  • Questions to artificial intelligence. If you use "Ask about this text", we send your question (up to 500 characters) and that day's devotional text to the Gemini service from Google to generate the answer. Lumi does not store the question or the answer; it stores only a daily count (at most 15 questions per day). Do not write personal or sensitive data in the question.
  • Phone notifications. The device identifier for notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging, from Google) and your language, to send circle notifications. A notification never contains the text of a prayer request.
  • Usage metrics. The event name and the day (for example "opened the app" or "finished a devotional"), tied to your account, at most once a day. Never the content of what you write.
  • Error reports. When the app fails, we send the error without identifying you, with anything between quotes, e-mails and links removed.
  • On your device. The app stores locally your sign-in, language, appearance, the Lumi items you chose and invites not yet opened.

We do not use ads, we do not sell data and we do not do advertising tracking.

3. Who the data goes through

  • Supabase (database and sign-in): stores your account data. Servers are in the United States.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications and Gemini for the artificial intelligence questions).
  • YouVersion (Bible reading and optional sign-in): the Bible tab uses the YouVersion platform, which handles data under its own policy.
  • GitHub (Android app distribution and hosting of the invite pages and this policy): GitHub sees the IP address of people opening those pages, and the app checks GitHub to know if there is a new version.
  • Device voice. The listen button uses your phone's voice. Some "more natural" voices need internet and may send the text to the system maker.

These services handle the data only to provide the service to Lumi. When data leaves Brazil it is because it is needed for the app to work, with the safeguards of the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, art. 33).

4. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while the account exists. Reflections, prayer requests and history stay until you delete them or delete the account. Error reports and metrics are kept only while they are useful to improve the app.

5. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18) you can ask for: confirmation that we process your data, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about who we share with, and to withdraw consent. You can also object to a processing activity.

To exercise any right, write to [CONTACT E-MAIL]. We answer within 15 days.

  • In the app itself you can change your name, language, reminders and notifications, delete a reflection in the history, leave a circle and give up a challenge.
  • Deleting your account and data: for now, ask by the e-mail above; deletion directly in the app is under development. When we delete, we erase your personal data. What is shared with other people, such as a circle created by you, will be transferred or removed in a way that does not harm the other members.

6. Security

We use a protected connection (HTTPS), per-account access control (each person reads only their own data and what is shared with them), abuse limits, and we keep the artificial intelligence key on the server only. No system is completely secure; if there is an incident that may affect you, we will notify you as the law requires.

7. Children and teenagers

Lumi is not aimed at children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to read this policy and follow your use.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change something important, we will tell you in the app and update the date at the top of this page.
