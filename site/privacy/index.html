Lumi Privacy Policy

Updated 13 Nov 2026 · Versão em português

Draft under review: the text will be reviewed before the store release. The periods in section 4 apply once automatic cleanup is switched on.

In a few lines

  • We do not sell your data and there are no ads.
  • Only you see what you write in your reflections. You can delete them in the app.
  • Prayer requests are seen only by the members of the circle where you shared them.
  • If you ask the artificial intelligence a question, the question and the devotional text go to Google.
  • To delete your account and data, use Settings > Account in the app or write to the contact e-mail.

1. Who we are

Lumi is a spiritual consistency app: a short daily devotional, reading plans, challenges and prayer circles with friends.

The party looking after your personal data (the "controller" in the law) is Wensch, who is responsible for Lumi. For any privacy matter, write to wensch22@gmail.com.

2. What data we use and why

  • Account. Your e-mail and the password you choose (the password is protected by the sign-in service; we never see it). If you sign in with YouVersion, we receive the e-mail of your YouVersion account. Used to create and access your account.
  • Profile. The name you choose, your age range, the reminder time, your time zone and your language. Used to personalize the app and the daily reminder.
  • Parental consent (children only). The name and e-mail of the parent or guardian who authorizes, the date and the version of the consent text.
  • Your activity. Devotionals started and finished, streak, XP, achievements, Lumi items, reading plans and challenges (including the ones you create).
  • Your reflections. The text you write at the end of the devotional is private: only you can see it. You can delete each reflection in your history. It is not part of the metrics and is not sent to artificial intelligence.
  • Circles. The circle name, your name as other members see it, the prayer requests you share and the "prayed" and "cheered" records. This is visible only to circle members. If someone reports content, we keep the report (who reported, who was reported and the reason) for moderation.
  • Challenge invites. People doing the same challenge with you see your name and the challenge progress (a count only, and whether it is finished). Whoever receives the invite code sees, before accepting, your name and the challenge.
  • Questions to artificial intelligence. If you use "Ask about this text", we send your question (up to 500 characters) and that day's devotional text to the Gemini service from Google to generate the answer. Lumi does not store the question or the answer; it stores only a daily count (at most 15 questions per day). Google may keep the question and the answer for a while, under the rules of the Gemini service. Do not write personal or sensitive data in the question.
  • Phone notifications. The device identifier for notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging, from Google) and your language, to send circle notifications. The notification carries your name (or the name of whoever acted) and the circle name, and goes through Google; it never contains the text of a prayer request.
  • Usage metrics. The event name and the day (for example "opened the app" or "finished a devotional"), tied to your account, at most once a day. Never the content of what you write.
  • Circle notices kept. We keep circle notices for 90 days (who prayed or cheered for you, and when), so you can see them in the app.
  • Abuse protection. We keep wrong invite-code attempts for 1 day, to prevent guessing. If a circle creator removes you, we keep that ban while the circle exists.
  • Error reports. When the app fails, we send the error without identifying you, removing anything between double quotes, curly quotes or backticks, e-mails, links and access codes.
  • On your device. The app stores locally your sign-in, language, appearance, the chosen voice, the Lumi items you chose, a device marker for notifications and invites not yet opened.

We do not use ads, we do not sell data and we do not do advertising tracking.

3. Who the data goes through

  • Supabase (database and sign-in): stores your account data. Servers are in the United States. It and Google keep technical connection logs (IP address and time).
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications and Gemini for the artificial intelligence questions).
  • YouVersion (Bible reading and optional sign-in): the Bible tab uses the YouVersion platform, which handles data under its own policy.
  • GitHub (Android app distribution and hosting of the invite pages and this policy): GitHub sees the IP address of people opening those pages (including an invite code, which is in the link address), and the app checks GitHub to know if there is a new version.
  • Device voice. The listen button uses your phone's voice. Some "more natural" voices need internet and may send the text to the system maker.

These services use the data only to provide the service to Lumi. When data leaves Brazil it is because it is needed for the app to work, with the safeguards of the Brazilian data protection law (LGPD, art. 33).

4. How long we keep data

We keep your account data while the account exists. Reflections, prayer requests and history stay until you delete them or delete the account. These data have a time limit and are deleted automatically:

  • Error reports: 60 days.
  • Wrong invite-code attempts: 1 day.
  • Usage metrics: 12 months.
  • Circle notices: 90 days.
  • Device notification identifiers: 30 days after becoming inactive (or 180 days unused).
  • Daily count of artificial intelligence questions: 7 days.
  • Abandoned or expired challenges: 180 days (finished ones stay).
  • Moderation reports: 12 months.

Provider backups may keep deleted data for a short period.

5. Your rights

Under the Brazilian data protection law (LGPD, art. 18) you can ask to: know whether we use your data, see it, correct it, make it anonymous, block or delete it, receive a copy, know who we share it with, and withdraw consent. You can also object to a use.

To exercise any right, write to wensch22@gmail.com. We answer within 15 days.

  • In the app itself you can change your name, language, reminders and notifications, delete a reflection in the history, leave a circle and give up a challenge.
  • Deleting your account and data: in the app, at Settings > Account > Delete my account and data, or by the e-mail above. When we delete, we erase your personal data, including the prayer requests, "prayed" and "cheered" you made. If you created a circle, it passes to its longest-standing member (or is removed if nobody else is in it); a challenge you created that others are doing passes to whoever started first. That way nobody loses what is theirs.

6. Security

We use a protected connection (HTTPS), per-account access control (each person reads only their own data and what is shared with them), abuse limits, and we keep the artificial intelligence key on the server only. No system is completely secure; if there is an incident that may affect you, we will notify you as the law requires.

7. Children and teenagers

Children: for a child to use Lumi, a parent or legal guardian must consent at sign-up, entering their own name and e-mail and ticking the authorization. We keep that name, that e-mail, the date and the version of the consent text. The guardian can ask, through the contact e-mail, to see, correct or delete the child's data.

Teenagers and other minors under 18: please ask a parent or guardian to read this policy and follow your use.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change something important, we will tell you in the app and update the date at the top of this page.
